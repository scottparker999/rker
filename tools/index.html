Tools

Small things that answer one question.

Calculators I wanted and couldn't find without a login attached. Each one runs entirely in your browser — no account, no tracking, and none of your numbers leave the page.

  • Operations

    Housekeeping labour model

    Your roster is built on occupancy. The work is created by departures. Models hours, staffing and cost per occupied room from the arrival and departure pattern — and prices what a shift in length of stay is actually worth.
  • Distribution

    OTA vs direct: true cost of a booking

    Commission is the part everyone quotes and the smallest part of the answer. Counts the Genius and member discounts, Preferred Partner uplift, sponsored ads, virtual card fees and cancellations — then tells you what you can afford to give away to win the booking direct.
  • Revenue

    Group displacement calculator

    Does this group contribute more than the transient business it pushes out? Night-by-night displacement, contribution after commission and cost, and the breakeven room rate.
