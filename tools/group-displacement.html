Tool

Group displacement calculator.

A group request is only worth what it contributes after the transient business it pushes out. This works that through night by night, on contribution rather than rate.

Method

Displacement is demand-limited, not size-limited. A group only displaces transient business where forecast demand would otherwise have filled those rooms. Thirty rooms into a soft Tuesday displaces nobody; the same thirty into a sold-out Saturday displaces thirty. That is why this is calculated per night — an aggregate view averages the peak away and quietly approves groups it shouldn't.

Contribution, not rate. A $200 transient room booked through an OTA at 15% with $28 of variable cost contributes $142. A $165 group room at 10% commission contributes $120.50. Comparing the headline rates says the gap is $35; the real gap is $21.50. Rate comparisons systematically overstate the cost of group business.

Ancillary counts. For most groups the rooms are not the whole trade. Meeting space and food and beverage are entered at the margin you keep, not the revenue you invoice, so they can be compared with room contribution on the same footing.

Wash is applied before the analysis. Blocks rarely pick up in full, so both the revenue and the displacement are calculated on expected materialised rooms. This assumes unsold rooms are released at cutoff and can still be sold to transient — if your contract holds inventory to arrival, set wash to zero.

The breakeven rate is the flat room rate at which the group exactly covers what it displaces, given everything else on this page. Below it you are buying occupancy with contribution. It is a floor for the negotiation, not a target.

What this deliberately does not model: rate dilution into future periods, the value of a repeat or multi-year relationship, contracted attrition and cancellation damages, length of stay shoulder effects, or the difference between displacing your lowest-rated transient and your average one. Those are judgement, and a calculator that pretends otherwise is worse than one that leaves the room for it.

Everything here runs in your browser. No figures are sent anywhere, nothing is stored, and there is no analytics on this page.
